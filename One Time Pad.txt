There is no single "equation" for deep purple in classical physics because purple is an extra-spectral color. Unlike pure violet, blue, or red, purple does not exist as a single wavelength of light on the electromagnetic spectrum.

Instead, purple is a mathematical construct generated by the human brain when the eye's short-wavelength (blue) and long-wavelength (red) cones are stimulated simultaneously, while the medium-wavelength (green) cones are bypassed.

However, we can define the exact mathematical coordinates and equations required to generate "deep purple" across different systems:
