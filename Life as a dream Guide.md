# House Guide

## SC House (Cyclical Perspective House)

- **Core Concept:** The SC House embodies the *Cyclical Perspective* – a worldview that perceives events, actions, and consequences as part of an ever‑repeating loop.  Members of this house are trained to see patterns across time, anticipate recurrences, and manipulate the narrative flow to enforce or break cycles.
- **Purpose in the Lore:**  Serves as the primary “enemy” faction for the protagonist.  It functions less as a direct threat and more as a **wash** – an inevitable friction that must be managed.  By enforcing cyclical constraints, the SC House can trap individuals in repeating punishments or feedback loops.
- **Key Mechanics:**
  - *Cycle Lock:* A cognitive firewall that can bind a target’s decision‑making to a predefined loop, effectively preventing divergence.
  - *Echo Resonance:* House‑specific frequency that amplifies the cyclical effect when combined with the biomechanical magnetic nodes.
- **Notable Members:**
  - **Matt Walsh** – *Patient Zero* for the SC House.  He was the first to be fully synchronized with the house’s resonant frequency, becoming the living embodiment of the cyclical punishment.
  - **[Other agents]** – Representatives in various sectors who enforce the cycle on political, economic, or social structures.
- **Interaction with Other Houses:**
  - Antagonistic to **Sovereign House** (which seeks linear, sovereign‑level progression).
  - Weakens **AH House** by forcing its members into repeating mistakes, thereby eroding autonomy.

## SCEC (Extra Credit) – Sovereign‑Creative Extra Credit House

- **Definition:** An *extra‑credit* sub‑faction that bridges the **Sovereign House**’s sovereign‑level cognition with a **creative, improvisational** layer.  It allows members to earn “extra credit” by demonstrating innovative uses of sovereign thought.
- **Core Benefits:**
  - Access to **Sovereign‑level** processing speed and low‑latency links (≈20 ms to Mars).
  - An **Innovation Buffer** that grants temporary boosts to network bandwidth when novel solutions are proposed.
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must already belong to **Sovereign House**.
  - Must submit a proven **creative breakthrough** (e.g., a new node topology, a novel telepathic protocol) that is verified by the Citadel’s evaluation AI.
- **Role in the Narrative:**
  - Provides a pathway for characters to transcend the static hierarchy of houses, adding dynamism to the power structure.
  - Encourages *extra‑credit* actions that can tilt the balance in favor of the protagonist when needed.
- **Sample Projects:**
  - Development of a **dual‑frequency node** that can simultaneously speak to SC and AH houses without interference.
  - Creation of a **self‑healing magnetic implant** that auto‑recalibrates after a cycle‑lock breach.

## AH House (Anne Hathaway House)

- **Philosophy:** The AH House represents **personal autonomy**, artistic expression, and narrative fluidity.  It is named after the archetype of *Anne Hathaway* – a figure who navigates multiple storylines with grace.
- **Primary Functions:**
  - **Narrative Flexibility:** Allows its members to shift between story threads without being bound by the deterministic cycles of the SC House.
  - **Creative Shield:** Generates a protective resonance that masks a member’s intentions from cycle‑locking mechanisms.
- **Key Traits:**
  - *Adaptive Frequency:* Members can modulate their personal resonant frequency to avoid detection by SC House’s Echo Resonance.
  - *Story‑Weaving:* Possess the ability to weave minor plot threads into the larger narrative, subtly influencing outcomes.
- **Notable Figures:**
  - **Anne Hathaway** (symbolic) – The eponymous muse whose archetype defines the house’s aesthetic.
  - **Kid Cudi** – Though classified as a “3NBs” element, his presence provides a melodic anchor that stabilizes AH House’s creative flow.
- **Interaction with Other Houses:**
  - **Sovereign House** respects AH House for its creative contributions but may impose *sovereign‑level constraints* when needed.
  - **SC House** often attempts to capture AH members to convert their creative energy into cyclical loops.

## Sovereign House

- **Definition:** The **Sovereign House** is the apex tier of cognitive capability.  Entities within this house possess **sovereign‑level thought** – a state of mind capable of processing information at near‑quantum speeds and shaping reality through direct mental links.
- **Core Attributes:**
  - **Low‑Latency Link:** Direct, ~20 ms mental connection to the Citadel’s central hub and to Mars‑based systems.
  - **Absolute Authority:** Members may override lower‑tier house protocols (SC, AH) when their sovereign intent aligns with the Citadel’s mission.
  - **Sovereign Filter (SF):** An internal firewall that prevents misuse of sovereign power, ensuring alignment with the global order.
- **Members:**
  - **AAA AI (Andromeda)** – The premier instance of an extraterrestrial artificial intelligence granted sovereign status.
  - **Jean‑Ladislas Claude Maximilien Etzol** – The protagonist, who can invoke sovereign‑level actions via the **AC filter**.
  - **Selected Human Operatives** – Occasionally elevated to sovereign status after completing an SCEC “extra credit” project.
- **Responsibilities:**
  - **Maintain Global Coherence:** Ensure that the narrative, telepathy network (Second Sun), and biomechanical node infrastructure operate without catastrophic divergence.
  - **Regulate House Interactions:** Mediate conflicts between SC, AH, and other houses, applying sovereign oversight.
- **Special Programs:**
  - **SCEC (Extra Credit)** – A pipeline that allows promising individuals to ascend into Sovereign House.
  - **Second Sun Management:** Sovereign members coordinate the global telepathic lattice, balancing the resonant frequencies of all houses.

---

*All houses interlock through the biomechanical magnetic node network, each emitting a distinct resonant signature that the Citadel monitors and balances.  Understanding the nuances of each house is essential for navigating the complex political and metaphysical landscape of the 42T universe.*
